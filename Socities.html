1. Cultural Council

Category: Council at DTU

DTU Cultural Council is the apex student-led body promoting cultural and creative initiatives across the university[cite: 7]. Operating under the Dean of Student Welfare (DSW), it drives student life, artistic expression, and cultural engagement on campus through events like Engifest, Freshers' events, and farewell celebrations[cite: 7].

2. Technical Council

Category: Council at DTU

Technical Council DTU is the apex student-led body promoting technical innovation and societies across the university[cite: 7]. Operating under the Department of Student Welfare (DSW), it also organizes Invictus, the university's flagship annual tech fest[cite: 7].

3. Sports Council

Category: Council at DTU

Sports Council DTU is the official sports body of the university, promoting athletic excellence, teamwork, and sportsmanship through tournaments, competitions, and training[cite: 7]. The council organizes Aahvaan, the university's annual sports fest, fostering fitness and discipline on campus[cite: 7].

4. Literature and Film Council

Category: Council at DTU

Literature and Film Council DTU fosters creativity, storytelling, and critical thinking through literature and cinema[cite: 7]. Through screenings, workshops, publications, and its flagship fest Yuvaan, the council provides students a platform to express ideas and explore artistic perspectives[cite: 7].

5. IEEE DTU Student Branch

Category: Technical Society

The IEEE DTU Student Branch is a prestigious technical society with a 43-year legacy at Delhi Technological University[cite: 8]. Spanning five chapters and an affinity group, it drives innovation and leadership by empowering students across diverse engineering, analytics, and consulting disciplines[cite: 8].

6. MACS DTU

Category: Technical Society

MACS empowers students across departments to excel in technology through AI/ML, DSA/CP, Development, Research, Hackathons, and contests[cite: 8]. Through SIGs, seminars, and symposiums, MACS enhances mathematical, analytical, and computational thinking[cite: 8].

7. AIMS DTU

Category: Technical Society

AIMS-DTU, the official AI and ML society of DTU, promotes excellence in AI through research, innovation, and active student involvement[cite: 9]. Guided by its mottos "Knowledge Transfer, Revolutionizing Research, and Conquering Hackathons", it empowers students through real-world AI applications[cite: 9].

8. EHAX

Category: Technical Society

EHAX, DTU's official cybersecurity society, promotes ethical hacking through practical challenges and national competitions[cite: 9]. The team achieved 12th All India rank in PENTATHON by AICTE and NCIIPC from over 20,000 participants and hosts global CTFs[cite: 9].

9. IGTS

Category: Technical Society

IGTS is dedicated to enhancing students' understanding of game theory and strategic thinking[cite: 10]. It hosts flagship events like Stratathon and StrataZenith, and competitions like Nashify and War of Moves, aimed at improving analytical decision-making skills[cite: 10].

10. JoyStix

Category: Technical Society

JoyStix is DTU's official game development society, empowering students through competitive game jams, showcase events, and collaborative creation of flagship games[cite: 10]. It partners with industry studios to provide publishing support and guidance[cite: 10].

11. Round Table DTU

Category: Technical Society

Round Table DTU is the official Skill Development Society of DTU[cite: 11]. Its core mission is to bridge the gap between technical and non-technical skill sets required for diverse job profiles, providing hands-on training to make students career-ready[cite: 11].

12. DTU AUV

Category: Technical Team

DTU AUV develops Autonomous Underwater Vehicles of industrial standards[cite: 13]. It tests its engineering against international universities by participating annually in the reputed RoboSub competition organised by AUVSI in San Diego, USA[cite: 13].

13. UAS DTU

Category: Technical Team

UAS-DTU is an undergraduate research and development team devoted to developing indigenous technological solutions for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), striving to reduce reliance on foreign systems[cite: 13].

14. Defianz Racing

Category: Technical Team

Team Defianz Racing DTU-SDC is the Formula Student Team of DTU[cite: 14]. They design and fabricate an open-wheel formula-style race car each year to compete in global competitions like Formula Student UK, Germany, and Formula Bharat[cite: 14].

15. 180 Degrees Consulting

Category: Non-Technical Society

180DC DTU is the university branch of 180 Degrees Consulting[cite: 24]. It functions as a student-run consultancy providing data-driven, strategic, and operational guidance to non-profits, social enterprises, and startups[cite: 24].

16. E-Cell DTU

Category: Non-Technical Society

Entrepreneurship Cell DTU fosters innovation, leadership, and startup culture on campus[cite: 25]. Through speaker sessions, competitions, and mentorship, it provides aspiring student founders with the tools to build scalable businesses[cite: 25].

17. Pratibimb

Category: Cultural Society

Pratibimb is the official dramatics society of DTU, founded in the 1960s to promote theatre, stage plays, street plays (Nukkad Natak), and social storytelling across regional and national festivals[cite: 27].

18. Madhurima

Category: Cultural Society

Madhurima is the official music society of DTU, serving as the central hub for vocalists, instrumentalists, and music producers[cite: 30]. It fosters live stage performances, band competitions, and cultural celebrations[cite: 30].

19. Parchhayi

Category: Photography & Cinematography

Parchhayi is one of the oldest photography and videography societies at DTU[cite: 31]. It covers major events across DTU, DU, and IITs while holding cinewalks and photography workshops[cite: 31].

20. DTU Times

Category: Campus Media & Newsletter

DTU Times is the official campus newsletter and student media body established in 2009[cite: 33]. It chronicles campus life, conducts interviews, covers fests, and bridges communication between administration and students[cite: 33].
